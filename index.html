    Take a Deep Breath

    You are not failing. Clinics navigate medication timing adjustments every single day.

    1. Call your clinic right away: State the exact medication and elapsed time.

    2. Do NOT double your dose unless your doctor explicitly confirms.

    3. Keep your medication box in hand when calling.

    4. Forgive yourself completely: One delayed dose does not erase your courage.

    🌱

    If the Plan Changes

    🌱 Slow Follicle Growth

    Follicles grow like flowers—some bloom faster, some take extra days. Clinics frequently add 2–3 stim days to optimize maturity.

    ❄️ Freeze-All Cycle

    If estrogen is elevated, a freeze-all avoids hyperstimulation (OHSS) and lets the uterine lining reset for transfer.

    💔 Paused Cycle

    A paused cycle is not a failure of your body—it is your medical team protecting your safety to begin fresh with a tailored plan.
